
### [[Pilot Testing]]

Running small-scale preliminary studies to refine methodology.

**Example:** Before launching a large national survey, researchers test their questionnaire with 30 participants to identify unclear questions, technical issues, or design flaws that might affect data quality.

### [[Response Validation]]

Checking for inconsistencies, outliers, and impossible values.

**Example:** In a health study, if a respondent reports being both 25 years old and having 30 years of work experience, this logical inconsistency flags the data for verification or exclusion.

### [[Non-Response Analysis]]

Understanding patterns in missing data.

**Example:** If a survey about workplace satisfaction shows that employees from a particular department consistently decline to participate, researchers would investigate whether this indicates a systematic issue that could bias results.
